# THE MERIDIAN: F-29 ALPHA / C-29 ECHO
## Locked Master Experience Plan

### Hidden truth
Fernanda Alpha unknowingly generates and sustains alternate realities through creativity. Stories, characters, paintings, imaginary people and possible futures become real somewhere beyond Alpha.

One of those realities contains another Fernanda Cruz: C-29 Echo.

Across realities, continuities have begun fragmenting through a phenomenon Meridian calls the Darkness. Meridian discovered that Alpha's creative output sustains possibility across these worlds. As creation stops, unfinished realities begin losing their futures.

Meridian does not want to stop this. It wants to control what fills the blank space after those worlds disappear.

Echo discovers the plan and crosses into Alpha to warn Fernanda.

Meridian labels Echo rogue and recruits Fernanda Alpha herself to perform a Correction.

---

## ACT I — Morning Directive
Three pieces of one Meridian letter are hidden:
- Coffee mug
- Bathroom/toothpaste
- Third discovery location

The fragments reconstruct a full directive.

The letter states:
- An unauthorized operative breached Alpha continuity at 02:17 AM.
- Target: C-29 ECHO.
- Echo has training comparable to Alpha and can anticipate investigative response.
- Evidence has therefore been dispersed.
- Echo is blamed for temporal fractures.
- Alpha is ordered to LOCATE, DETERMINE OBJECTIVE, and CORRECT the target.
- Alpha is forbidden from communicating with Echo.

A riddle in the directive leads to the hidden QR code for the Alpha Corrections network.

## ACT II — Meridian Website
Fernanda accesses:
1. Target Profile
2. Surveillance
3. Fracture Analysis
4. Intercept 02-17
5. Correction Authorization
6. Restricted Archive

The site does not reveal that Echo is another Fernanda.

### Surveillance anomalies
Normal reference images show:
- Two-hands painting upright
- Junk closet game order
- Bottom-right art loft picture upright

Reality differs:
- Painting flipped upside down
- Two games swapped
- Art loft picture flipped upside down

Each location yields:
- Δ
- 29
- ORIGIN

The closet anomaly uses UNO. Fernanda must identify the swap and play UNO to earn the `29` fragment.

---

### Alpha activation order
Meridian explains why F-29 Alpha was activated instead of a standard field team:
- Echo repeatedly anticipates Meridian investigative procedure.
- F-29 Alpha has the highest predictive match to Echo's behavior.
- The breach occurred inside Alpha's immediate environment, giving her access to context and relationships unavailable to outside teams.
- Alpha's objectives are to verify the breach, recover dispersed evidence, determine Echo's objective, identify contacted individuals, evaluate the fracture connection, and complete the Correction if hostile intent is confirmed.

## ACT III — Contradiction
The fracture log contains many entries.

Key hidden contradiction:
- Darkness is detected in Alpha before Echo arrives.
- Alpha fractures before Echo arrives.
- Echo breaches only at 02:17.
- Meridian nevertheless identifies Echo as the cause.

The damaged audio intercept also contains:
- "Alpha..."
- "not the source..."
- "before I arrived..."
- "three..."
- "can't let Meridian..."
- "find me..."

Fernanda is required to acknowledge that she will not communicate with Echo.

## ACT IV — Archive Hijack
Fernanda enters:

Δ / 29 / ORIGIN

The Meridian archive appears to malfunction.

Echo hijacks the connection.

Echo reveals:
- Her real name is Fernanda Cruz.
- She is another Fernanda from another continuity.
- She crossed into Alpha to warn her.
- Meridian monitors complete transmissions.
- She divided the next message across three trusted people.

Echo proves her identity with a private detail:
Fernanda still has people who exist nowhere anyone else can see. Kevin knows they exist, but there are parts of that private world she keeps entirely her own.

Echo adds:
"Mine have different names."

## ACT V — Three Friends
Echo contacted:
- FIRST: Nakeyah — best friend
- SECOND: Joao — lives in Brazil
- THIRD: Jay — sister/art connection

Each receives a personal Echo letter.

Each letter encodes part of the melody of "Somewhere Over the Rainbow" in a different way:
- FIRST: anomalous capitals
- SECOND: anomalous lowercase letters
- THIRD: acrostic or another pattern

Letters are written backward from a verified playable melody.

Fernanda combines FIRST → SECOND → THIRD and plays the notes on the piano.

She recognizes "Somewhere Over the Rainbow."

## ACT VI — Rainbow
A printed rainbow has been taped near the bed since earlier in the day.

Fernanda realizes the instruction is not just RAINBOW but OVER THE RAINBOW.

High on the ceiling above it is an envelope containing:
- Latitude coordinate
- A clue pointing to Riverwalk

The 20-foot ceiling forces use of the ladder.

The investigation becomes a birthday outing.

## ACT IX — Meridian Agenda
Echo does NOT yet reveal the Darkness.

Echo reveals:
- Meridian already knows what the Darkness is.
- Meridian is not trying to stop it.
- Meridian wants worlds to collapse so it can control what replaces them.
- Echo knows who can stop the Darkness: Fernanda.
- While Fernanda was away, Echo routed one object through the normal delivery network.
- Echo's final message is inside.
- Meridian is tracking Alpha through her phone.

Fernanda is instructed:
POWER OFF THE PHONE.
GO HOME.
FIND WHAT WASN'T THERE WHEN YOU LEFT.

## ACT X — Chair
A boxed new computer chair is waiting in the package area.

Echo claims she "shipped" it into Alpha through normal delivery.

Fernanda and Kevin bring it inside and open it.

Hidden with the chair is a flash drive.

The flash drive contains:
- Echo's final letter
- A blank Word document

The chair is assembled before the final action.

---

## ACT XI — Darkness Reveal
Echo explains:

The Darkness is not a creature or weapon.

It is what remains when creation stops.

Every world Fernanda imagines exists somewhere.
Every character lives.
Every painting extends beyond the canvas.
Every imaginary person belongs to some possibility.
Every alternate future continues somewhere.

Echo herself is proof.

Fernanda has not failed these worlds; she has simply been busy living her own life. Echo's message is encouragement, not guilt.

Meridian discovered the connection and intends to exploit the empty worlds.

Echo was not invading Alpha.
She was trying to reach the person who created hers.

---

## ACT XII — Final Solution
The second file on the flash drive is blank.

Echo explains:
- Meridian could not write it.
- Echo could not write it.
- Only Alpha can.
- Fernanda does not have to finish everything.
- She does not have to save every reality tonight.
- She only needs to create something.

She sits in her new chair.
Opens the blank document.
Writes what comes next.

End.
